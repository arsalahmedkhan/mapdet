Hamtramck Sanborn 1949-1951 - window 1 sample (work in progress)

HOW TO OPEN: unzip the folder, then double-click index.html (Chrome or Edge). No internet is needed.

WHAT IT SHOWS: the old map with a draft layer (not checked by people): an outline and a centre point for each building, house numbers where they could be read,
a draft street (nearest OpenStreetMap street line; map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors), a material guess, and (switch it on) the hand-made buildings from the Michigan Tech project to compare. Zoom in to see the sharp level-20 map tiles.
Click a building for details. Tick 'Review mode' to correct a building (number, street, verdict), add a missing building and download your corrections as a CSV; they are saved in your browser only. Orange or "(check)" means the draft is unsure.

LIMITS: window 1 only (about 0.6 square km); house numbers are partly wrong or missing (about three in four right when a number is given, and many buildings have none yet); street names come from OpenStreetMap street lines (about nine in ten right on the checked buildings, mostly wrong for some buildings on Joseph Campau).
Map tiles and hand-made buildings: Michigan Tech Hamtramck Historic Spatial Archaeology Project (original Sanborn sheets: Library of Congress). Please credit them.
No pupil data is in this folder.
